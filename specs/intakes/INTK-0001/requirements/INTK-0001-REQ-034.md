All automatic follow-up work and its resulting tasks shall remain inside the
maintenance workspace so that the operational picture is complete without users
checking a second system.

## Acceptance scenarios

```gherkin
Scenario: Keep follow-up in one workspace
  Given automatic follow-up work and its tasks
  When they are created
  Then they remain inside the maintenance workspace
```
